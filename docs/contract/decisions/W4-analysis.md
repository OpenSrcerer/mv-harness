## Interpretations

- W4-AN-I1 (W4A-ACAP test): `len(kicks) <= 2 or qa:kick*` is met two ways: the formant gate leaves 1 mix event, and the
  kickless stem then gives `kick_source = drums` with 0 kicks.
- W4-AN-I2 (S18): the reviewers' options were "a choreo re-vote with a new field" or "drums in rhythm's deps". The
  tests read `meta/tempo.json` (rhythm's file), which choreo cannot write; a DAG change is frozen. The backbeat is audible
  in the mix bar-synchronously (rap / guitar onsets average out), so rhythm votes it itself.
- W4-AN-I3: the adversary perturbation folders (`out/_wip/r-adversary/<name>`) and the W3 critic folders
  (`out/_wip/integrator/<slug>`) are read at fixed paths by the review tests; the fixer validated on copies in
  `out/_wip/w4fix-analysis/<name>` (see the report for how to point the tests at them).
