# M4 decisions (harmony, mood)

Contract decisions and interpretations taken by builder-m4. Nothing here changes a frozen field; every addition is optional.

## Harmony

- **H1 Chord labels keep lv-chordia's spelling, inversions go to `bass_pc`.** `label` is `<root>:<quality>` with the root spelled as lv-chordia
  emits it (`Bb:maj`), never with a `/bass` suffix; `root_pc`, `quality` and `bass_pc` carry the numbers. `D:maj/5` becomes label `D:maj`,
  `bass_pc` 9. Qualities come from the contract vocabulary (maj, min, 7, maj7, min7, dim, aug, sus2, sus4, dim7, hdim7, 9, maj9, min9, 11, 13);
  Harte shapes outside it degrade to maj or min. `N` has `root_pc`, `bass_pc` null and `quality` `N`.
- **H2 The chord timeline is gapless over [0, duration).** Gaps and the tail become `N`; every chord boundary is moved to the nearest beat
  (at most half the local beat interval away; `snap_max_beat_frac`), segments that collapse onto the same beat are dropped and equal
  neighbours merge. `beat_index` is the beat the chord starts on, null when the boundary has no beat within 35 % of the interval.
- **H3 `bass_pc` is verified against a bass chroma (C1..G3 of the harmonic part).** lv-chordia's inversion is kept when the bass line confirms
  it; otherwise the dominant bass pitch class is used when it is a chord tone (share >= 0.30) or a clear slash note (>= 0.55), else the root.
- **H4 Chord `conf` is not lv-chordia's.** lv-chordia returns no per-segment probability. `conf` = 0.6 x chroma evidence (the chosen root against
  the best other root, weighted 0.7, plus the chosen quality family against the other one, 0.3) + 0.4 x agreement with an independent
  template + Viterbi decode over the segment's beats. For `N` it is how un-chord-like the chroma is.
- **H5 Fallback `chords:template`.** When lv-chordia fails (or `--param` removes it) the beat-synchronous template + sticky Viterbi decode
  becomes the chord timeline (triads only), the stage status is `degraded` and `fallbacks` contains `chords:template`. The same decode
  always runs as the cross-check.
- **H6 Key ensemble.** Evidence sources, each z-scored (clipped at +-3) and summed: S-KEY (log probability; global pass and the mean of the
  30 s windows), Krumhansl-Schmuckler, Temperley and Albrecht-Shanahan profiles on the harmonic chroma, a diatonic fit of the chord timeline
  (tonic chord bonus, first/last chord bonus), a bass-line fit, and a **phrase-start anchor** (the chord every 4th bar starts on). Weights:
  S-KEY 1.0, chords 1.0, phrase 0.7, KS 0.45, Temperley 0.35, Albrecht 0.3, bass 0.4. The S-KEY softmax is never used as a confidence.
- **H7 Relative-key tie-break.** When the two leading keys are relative (same pitch set, e.g. C major / A minor), their ensemble scores are
  within 3.0 and the phrase-start evidence favours the other one by more than 0.25, the one the phrases start on wins by 0.05. The
  vi-IV-I-V loop of the synthetic song is read as A minor this way; the ambiguity is always flagged (`key:relative_ambiguity`) and the
  other key is listed under `meta/key.json` `methods.ensemble.alternatives`.
- **H8 Key confidence** = 0.40 x window agreement (fraction of 30 s window winners equal to the global key, a relative key counts half) +
  0.35 x method credit (each independent method's top-1 against the winner: exact 1, relative 0.55, parallel or fifth-neighbour 0.30) +
  0.25 x ensemble margin; capped at 0.5 when S-KEY and the chord model name unrelated keys. A forced key (`harmony.key_override`) has conf 1.
- **H9 `meta/key.json.agreement`** is the weakest relation any independent method has to the winner (`agree`, `relative`, `parallel`, `fifth`
  = pitch sets one step apart on the circle of fifths, or `disagree` when any method names an unrelated key). `events/key.json` `agree` is
  true only for `agree`. `flags`: `key:relative_ambiguity`, `key:parallel_ambiguity`, `key:fifth_ambiguity`, `key:methods_disagree`,
  `key:models_disagree`, `key:skey_differs`, `key:low_confidence`, `key:modulation`, `key:overridden`.
- **H10 Key regions** come from a sticky Viterbi over 30 s windows (hop 5 s; switch costs 9 nats to a parallel/fifth neighbour, 12 to an
  unrelated key, +1 home bonus), regions under 20 s are absorbed, boundaries snap to the nearest downbeat within 4 s. A relative key is never
  a key change (same pitch set): it is folded into the global key.
- **H11 Additive optional fields** in `meta/key.json`: `methods.<name>.top` (top-3 per method), `methods.skey.window_agreement`,
  `methods.ensemble{weights, window_agreement, method_credit, margin, n_windows, alternatives[]}`. No item field was added to `events/chords.json`
  or `events/key.json`.
- **H12 `curves/chroma12.f32`**: HPSS (librosa harmonic, margin 2) then a CQT (C1..B7, 3 bins per semitone, tuning estimated), pitch classes
  from C2 up, linearly interpolated from the 86 Hz analysis grid to 100 Hz, max-normalised per frame; frames more than 45 dB under the song's
  p95 harmonic energy are 0.
- **H13 Human-correction hook** (no contract change): stage param `key_override` (`--param harmony.key_override="\"A minor\""`) forces the global key.
  A per-chord edit hook needs an `overrides.json` section (change request CR-M4-1).

## Mood

- **M1 Scale.** emoMusic and DEAM output (valence, arousal) on 1..9; `(x - 1) / 8` maps to 0..1 (0.5 neutral). `mood.valence` / `mood.arousal` are the
  mean of the two heads. Per-head values are exposed as optional fields `valence_emomusic`, `valence_deam`, `arousal_emomusic`, `arousal_deam`
  (events items) and as `heads` in `meta/mood.json`.
- **M2 Confidence.** arousal: 0.80 - 2.5 x head gap - 1.0 x within-section std (0.05..0.9). valence: 0.45 - 1.5 x head gap - 0.5 x std, capped at 0.5.
  Spans under 3 live seconds are x 0.8. Digital-silence seconds (RMS < -70 dBFS) read 0.5 / confidence 0 / tags empty.
- **M3 `mood.tag.<tag>`** writes all 56 MTG-Jamendo classes (not only the top few); `events/mood.json` `tags` are the top 3 above p 0.08 per section
  (at least one), with the optional aligned `tag_p` array. `meta/mood.json.global.tags` are the top 8.
- **M4 Curve timing.** msd-musicnn patches (3.0 s, hop 1.5 s) and discogs-effnet patches (2.06 s, hop 1.0 s) are placed at their audio-span centre and
  linearly interpolated to 1 Hz, sample i centred at i s (clamped before the first and after the last centre).
- **M5 `meta/mood.json`** gains optional `heads`, `flags` (`mood:valence_low_confidence` always; `mood:valence_heads_disagree` when the heads
  differ by more than 0.12 on the whole song; `mood:arousal_heads_disagree`) and `arc` (min/max of the 1 Hz curves).

## Change requests (for the W3 integrator)

- **CR-M4-1** `overrides.json` has no `harmony` or `mood` section. A viewer correction for the key or for a chord would need
  `harmony.key` (string) and `harmony.chords` (time-anchored edits) in `OVERRIDES` and `STAGE.override_keys`. Until then the key hook is the `key_override` param.
- **CR-M4-2** `docs/CONTRACT.md` lists a `mean_db` field under `chords` that the catalogue does not declare (a generated-table artefact);
  harmony does not write it.

## Fixer round (fixer-m4)
- S-KEY export is softmax(softmax(.)): `keys.recover_probs` inverts it (p = log q - mean(log q) + 1/24). meta/key.json methods.skey gains `p_max`; the old "flat" note is replaced. S-KEY windows with p_max < 0.2 or < 20 % unmasked frames do not vote; the vote weight scales with p_max (full at 0.6). Score fed to the ensemble is p (linear), not log p.
- Key confidence is capped at 0.25 with flags `key:no_tonal_evidence` + `key:low_confidence` when fewer than two independent non-anchor methods have evidence (additive flag).
- Chroma: tuning passed to librosa.cqt is scaled by BPO/12 (semitone fractions -> CQT-bin fractions).
