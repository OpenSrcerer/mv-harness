# M2 (Rhythm) contract decisions and interpretations

Module M2 owns allin1, rhythm, structure, choreo and cues (builder-m2, W2). Everything below is either additive
(optional fields / keys the stage writes on top of the frozen catalogue) or an interpretation of the contract where it
was silent. Frozen fields are written exactly as catalogued. Change requests for the W3 integrator are listed at the end.

## Additive fields (minor version; optional, documented in each envelope's `fields`)

| File | Field | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `allin1.json` (cache only) | `bpm_from_beats` | median-IBI tempo of allin1's own beats (its integer `bpm` can be 2 BPM off) |
| `allin1.json` | `embeddings_layout`, `model` | `stems_concat:bass,drums,other,vocals`; model name, folds, package version |
| `events/bars.json` | `irregular` (bool, only when true) | a bar that ended early (meter HMM "break") |
| `events/tempo_segments.json` | `feel` | normal / half_time / double_time for the segment (trackers' local tempo vs the grid) |
| `meta/tempo.json` | `arbitration` | chosen tempo, margin, ambiguity, the scored candidate rows (consensus, metrical contrast/coverage, salience, prior), tempogram peaks |
| `meta/tempo.json` | `downbeat_vote` | voters and, per tracker, downbeat F vs the fused bars and the best phase shift (-2..+2 beats) |
| `meta/tempo.json` | `trackers`, `pairwise_beat_f`, `tracker_lag` | per-tracker tempo, F vs the grid, beat count; pairwise beat F; per-tracker latency vs attacks |
| `meta/tempo.json` | `feel_shares`, `pulse`, `refinement`, `override_hint` | share of the song per feel; pulse statistics; snapping statistics; suggested `rhythm.bpm` / `rhythm.downbeat_phase` alternatives for the viewer |
| `events/sections.json` | `energy` (z), `edm_role` (or null) | section drive (bass + kick + level, z-scored) and the EDM role whose segment starts in it |

## Interpretations

- **M2-D1 Sections start at bar 1.** The pickup bar (bar 0) belongs to no section (as in the synthetic truth). The
  last section ends at the song end. When the beatless intro is >= 4 beats long, rhythm writes virtual beats back to
  t >= 0 so bar 1 starts within one bar of 0.
- **M2-D2 Virtual beats (D11).** Internal no-pulse gaps get a uniform grid between the surrounding pulse beats;
  after the last pulse beat the grid continues to the end (stops 0.25 beat before it); leading no-pulse beats are
  dropped unless the intro is >= 4 beats, and pickup beats are recovered backwards where an attack supports them.
  Virtual beats carry `conf` 0.5.
- **M2-D3 allin1 embeddings** are stored as the 4 stems concatenated (768 dims) and mean-pooled to 10 Hz
  (consumers read `rate_hz` and `dim`). The W0 real fixtures hold the 100 Hz stem mean (dim 192); both are read.
- **M2-D4 Tracker latency calibration.** Each tracker's activation and beats are shifted by its median offset to the
  physical attacks (transients >= 15 dB rise, else 12/9 dB) before fusion. On the synthetic song this recovers
  beat_this -20 ms, allin1 +10 ms, madmom 0 ms exactly; on the real songs the offsets are a few ms.
- **M2-D5 Downbeat vote.** The three voters are beat_this (its own peak-picked downbeats), allin1 (its decoded
  downbeats) and madmom's DBN **re-decoded at the arbitrated tempo +-20 %** (madmom's free DBN can sit an octave
  away). Votes plus the mean downbeat activation feed a meter HMM over beats (3/4, 4/4; switch and early-bar costs
  scale with the median downbeat evidence). A tracker whose best phase shift differs from 0 by a clear margin is
  flagged `downbeat_phase_off:<tracker>:<shift>` (found on versatile and newton-vs-nye for madmom, as in the SPIKE).
- **M2-D6 Tempo octave.** Chosen by consensus + drum metrical evidence + tempogram salience + a 120 BPM log-normal
  prior; all candidates are kept with `relation` and a score normalised to the winner. `override_hint["rhythm.bpm"]`
  lists the alternatives with score >= 0.6 plus the x2 / x0.5 levels.
- **M2-D7 Feel** per segment: `half_time` / `double_time` when the majority of the trackers with beats in a +-8-beat
  window run at half / double the grid tempo; runs shorter than 8 beats revert to normal. `meta/tempo.json.feel` is
  the dominant one by duration.
- **M2-D8 Section labels** use allin1's Harmonix probabilities when verse+chorus carry >= 30 % of the mass
  (informative), shared inside a repeat group. Otherwise (electronic, instrumental) structural labels:
  intro / build / drop / breakdown / inst / break / outro, with `build`/`drop`/`breakdown` labels only where
  `events/edm_roles.json` has that role. `label_probs` is always allin1's distribution (mapped start -> intro,
  end -> outro).
- **M2-D9 Tier-4 cues** are written only for in-pulse beats (virtual beats in no-pulse regions are not cut points);
  `kinds[]`/`sources[]` are written only when a cue merges several candidates (they are optional; `kinds[0] == kind`
  holds whenever present). This keeps cues.json about 25 % smaller than the fixture.
- **M2-D10 Cue snapping.** Impacts, stops, silences, hit points and pins within 50 ms of a beat are moved onto the
  beat so they merge with the downbeat/beat cue (D37 merges exactly co-timed candidates only).
- **M2-D11 Lead-ins.** Section / build / breakdown lead-in = one nominal bar (beats_in_bar x local beat interval);
  drop lead-in = drop start - start of the build that ends within two bars of it (else one bar).
- **M2-D12 Hit points** follow the synthetic truth's formula (>= 3 coinciding kinds among drum, cymbal(crash),
  impact, vocal_onset, chord change on a downbeat, section, drop; stops always). Cymbals that repeat on the grid
  (>= 3 within +-2 beats) are treated as ride (hat row, not a hit-point kind); isolated ones as crash. When the drums
  stage is unavailable, `accent` (top-10 % beat accent) and `onset` (strong spectral onset) also count. At most
  max(24, 0.35 x bars) are kept, ranked by strength.
- **M2-D13 Pinned cues** are tier 1, strength 1.0, source `{file: "overrides.json", id: "cues.pinned:<k>"}`.
- **M2-D14 E000** covers the 4 beats before E001 (3 in a 3/4 start, counted 4-5-6); its accents are the existing
  beats' accents, 0 for extrapolated beats; `in_pulse` is true only if all four beats exist and are in pulse.
- **M2-D15 Fallbacks** (recorded in `StageResult.fallbacks` and provenance notes): `chroma:own_cqt`,
  `dynamics:unavailable`, `drums:own_hpss` (choreo's own percussive band classifier), `intensity:own_rms`,
  `<tracker>:unavailable`, `rhythm:dsp_only`, `models:disabled` (param `use_models=false`).

- **M2-D16 QA flags** in `meta/tempo.json.qa.flags`: `tempo_ambiguous` (arbitration margin small),
  `tempo_trackers_disagree` (the tracker tempi are not even octave-related, e.g. MEGAFAUNA 105 / 162 / 194: the
  arbitrated level is evidence-based but the user should confirm `rhythm.bpm`; also a stage warning),
  `low_tracker_agreement` (median tracker F vs the grid < 0.7), `downbeat_phase_off:<tracker>:<shift>`,
  `feel_changes`, `no_pulse_regions` / `no_pulse`, `irregular_bars`, `tracker_missing:<tracker>`.

- **M2-D17 Section merging and drop context.** Adjacent sections in the same repeat group merge while the merged
  section stays <= `merge_repeat_max_bars` (8) bars and no user-forced boundary separates them (4 + 4 bars of one
  loop is one danceable section). A drop's bass/kick jump is measured against the `drop_context_bars` (2) non-silent
  bars right before it, so an instrument entering earlier in the previous section is not mistaken for a drop.

## Change requests (W3 integrator)

- **CR M2-1 `moments.d`.** The catalogue declares `d` as required `number|null`, but the events envelope
  (`mvh/envelope/events`) types any present `d` as a number, so `null` can never validate. cues writes `d = 0.0`
  for instantaneous moments. Fix: make the envelope's `d` nullable for `kind: event`, or make `moments.d` a number.
- **CR M2-2 Stub matrices.** `run_stub` copies cache-only matrices (`activations.f32`) with `writer.copy`, so the
  done record has no `matrices` entry and `Upstream.matrix("activations")` fails for stubs; M2 reads the raw file
  with the layout from `allin1.json` (`mvh.stages.allin1.read`). A stub that calls `writer.matrix` would make the
  intermediate uniform.
- **CR M2-3 madmom-infer 0.2.0** `RNNBeatTrackingObservationModel.log_densities` calls `np.asarray(..., ndmin=1)`,
  which numpy 2.5 rejects; rhythm subclasses it with madmom's original densities. Upstream fix or a vendored patch.
- **CR M2-5 Obsolete W1a test.** `tests/unit/contract/test_catalog_dag.py::test_stub_files_are_stubs` asserts that
  `mvh.stages.rhythm.run` calls `run_stub`; it fails now that rhythm is real and makes the gate's unit step fail for
  every module (tests/unit/contract is in every gate). It should pick a stage that is still a stub, or check the
  stub mechanism (`mvh.contract.fixture.run_stub`) directly. Not M2's path; not edited.
- **CR M2-6 Context-cheap cue view.** With every frozen field required, cues.json is 63-118 KB on the real songs
  (about 200 bytes per cue; tier-4 beat references are 63-76 % of the cues even after M2-D9). Proposal: an optional
  `events/cues.major.json` (tiers 1-3 only, same schema) or making the tier-4 nullable fields optional, so agents can
  load the cut list without the per-beat references (which events/beats.json already holds).
- **CR M2-4 (info)** `tables/sections.csv` `level`/`mean_lufs` and the impact/stop/silence cues need the dynamics
  stage; on the real fixtures (no dynamics) they are null / absent until M1 lands.

## Review round 1 (fixer-m2)
- **M2-D-pin** A user pin (`cues.pinned`) keeps its identity after the D37 merge: the cues stage moves `pinned` to the
  front of `kinds[]`/`sources[]` and sets `kind = pinned`, `source` = the override source; strength stays 1.0, lead-in
  and strength are nulled only for tier-4 cues, and moments are selected from `kinds[]`.
  **CR M2-7 (W3)**: move `pinned` to the front of `CUE_KIND_PRIORITY` (the stage works around it locally).
- **M2-D-short** Short inputs: rhythm (metrical_evidence window clipped to the song, tempogram detrend) and choreo
  (smoothing) use a length-preserving `smooth_same`; a 1 s song passes rhythm, structure and choreo.
- **M2-D-minbars** Sections are never shorter than `min_section_bars` (2) except forced pieces (silence runs, user
  boundaries) and a short tail; the short-first-section path is removed.
- **M2-D-melody** Repeat groups also use a melodic fingerprint (chroma of 4 sub-bar slices minus the bar mean:
  static harmony cancels), section similarity = max(main, melody). A boundary with score >= `merge_keep_strong` (1.4)
  x cost is not merged away by the same-group merge.
- **M2-D-phase** Downbeat vote: a tracker whose downbeats sit a constant shift off the first-pass fused bars
  (phase_report best_shift, F gain > 0.2) votes with the shift removed, then the meter is re-decoded. QA flags
  (`downbeat_phase_off:*`) still report the raw tracker phase.

## Review round 2 (fixer-m2-r2)
- **M2-D-struct-evidence** structure boundary score: own evidence = allin1 boundary / segments + damped (first/last bars)
  signal novelty (combined SSM, drum / onset-pattern novelty `w_pattern`, allin1 embedding novelty `w_embedding`, energy
  change) + silence edges. The dynamics stage's segments, impacts and `dyn.novelty` are weak confirmatory evidence
  (gated by the bar's own evidence, `dyn_gate`), not boundary sources. The synthetic fixture's dynamics segments equal
  the truth sections, so tests must not rely on them.
- **M2-D-grid-split** A section of >= 8 bars is split on the 4-bar grid at 4-bar-window novelty >= `split_min_novelty`
  (0.5, p98-normalised); user `structure.boundaries` removals are respected.
- **M2-D-agglo** Repeat groups: agglomerative average linkage (threshold unchanged); neighbouring sections' similarity is
  scaled by `repeat_adjacent_penalty` (0.85); the unequal-length penalty exponent is `repeat_len_exp` (0.15).
- Additive: `meta/structure.json` carries `boundary_components` (per-bar own evidence terms).

## W3 module fix round 1 (fixer-m2-r3): F4-r3, F6-r3, seams S05, S09
- **M2-D-jitter** The bar grid is an estimate (downbeats within +-20 ms are correct by the rhythm thresholds), so the
  structure stage must not change its answer on a frame or two of downbeat jitter:
  - `features.bar_pattern` slots are centred on the 16th-note grid positions (grid point +- half a slot), not bounded
    by them; a downbeat up to half a slot late keeps the same fingerprint.
  - The novelty terms (combined-SSM Foote novelty, energy change, drum-pattern and embedding novelty) and the
    phrase-grid refinement's 4-bar novelty are averaged over bar grids shifted by -`bar_jitter_frames`..+`bar_jitter_frames`
    (2 frames = +-20 ms). Repeat groups and energy use the unshifted grid.
  - `segment_dp` charges a section shorter than 4 bars that does not end at a forced boundary or at the song's end an
    extra `short_section_margin` (0.25) x section cost: a phrase-breaking cut needs a clear margin over the cost.
- **M2-D-impact-snap** A major impact (dynamics `impacts`, strength >= `impact_snap_strength` 0.6) on the downbeat one bar
  before a section start moves that start back to the impact bar when the impact bar clears the section cost on its own
  (the band enters a bar before the voice; allin1's functional boundary follows the voice). `0` disables it. Recorded in
  provenance notes.
- **M2-D-drop-extent** A drop runs on while the following sections stay on the plateau: drive within 0.6 z of the drop's
  plateau level (mean bar drive over its first 8 non-silent bars, independent of how the opening is segmented), or
  within 1.2 z when the section repeats material already in this drop (same repeat group). A section of a drop's repeat
  group with its own drop cue (bass/kick jump, silence/stop before, or impact + rise) at the drop's energy is a drop
  even when it sits just below the song's 70th-percentile drive (evidence cites "repeats the drop at ...").
- **M2-D-form-labels** Allin1-informative labelling gets two form rules: the opening non-silent section is `intro` when
  no other section repeats it and its drive is below the song median; a section that nothing repeats, sits between two
  `chorus` sections and that allin1 cannot place (no Harmonix class >= 0.55) is `bridge`. `label_probs` still carries
  allin1's probabilities; the relabelling is noted in provenance.
- Additive: `meta/structure.json` `boundary_components.nov_grid` (the jitter-averaged 4-bar grid novelty).

## W3 module fix round 2 (fixer-m2-r4): F7-r4
- **M2-D-phrase-grid** Phrases restart at every section start, but a section that is not a whole number of phrases keeps
  the SONG's phrase grid: its remainder becomes a leading (pickup) phrase when the other section starts clearly sit on
  the grid through `start + remainder` (support = other starts on the phrase_L grid count 1, on the 4-bar grid only 0.5;
  the song start excluded; needs >= half of the other starts and a margin of 1 over the plain section start), else the
  remainder closes the section as before. A section start snapped one bar back to an impact (M2-D-impact-snap) therefore
  gets a 1-bar pickup phrase and the eight-counts (choreo) stay on the grid of the original boundary: Jeremy verse#1 is
  P 19.47 s (1 bar, partial 4-beat eight-count) + P 21.80 s (8 bars), count 1 on the vocal entry. Pure function
  `structure.phrase_layout(sec_ranges, phrase_L)`; provenance note "phrase remainder placed at the section start".
  On the other 3 real songs and the synthetic song no phrase changes. The pickup eight-count keeps `first_count` 1
  (CONTRACT: first_count is 1, or 5 only for E000).
