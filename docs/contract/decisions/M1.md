# M1 (Signal) contract decisions: spectral, loudness, dynamics

Owner: builder-m1 · contract v1.0.0-rc.1 · all items are **additive** (optional fields / one optional file) or
interpretations of frozen fields; nothing frozen was renamed, removed or retyped. Items that need a contract or runtime
change are listed at the end as change requests.

## spectral

| # | Decision | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| M1-S1 | Band "dBFS" (`bands.*_db`, `fine.*_db`, `hits.*.db`) = 10·log10(mean square) of the band, from the mid **and** side STFT power (= the mean over L/R); a full-scale sine reads -3.01 dBFS (same convention as the fixture and `loud.rms_db`). The viz-compatible values (`bands.*_viz`, `bands.level`, `spectrum128`, the hit envelopes) use the mono down-mix only, as the Web Audio analyser does. | interpretation |
| M1-S2 | Faithful viz port: Web Audio periodic Blackman (α 0.16), `|X|/N`, per-bin dB, band map, 2.6 dB/oct tilt, -88..-18 dB, soft knee, band smoothing 22/110 ms, energies 30/140 ms, `level`, `_hitEnvelope`, `_flux`, flux multipliers/floor/gaps/gates and strength formulas. Verified against the real `viz/js/audio.js` run in Node (`tests/unit/spectral/viz_parity.cjs`). Analyser size 2048 at 44.1/48 kHz (4096 at 88.2/96 kHz). | interpretation |
| M1-S3 | Offline parts (PLAN §5): centred 100 Hz frames; per-song gain `clamp(0.95 / max(p99.5(max_k raw_k), 0.2), 0.6, 3.5)` instead of the AGC (recorded in the spectrum128 matrix meta as `gain`); hit reference = centred ±8 s median of attack sizes; flux threshold = centred 0.8 s mean (current frame excluded). | interpretation |
| M1-S4 | Kick gate = `max(p95 - 30, -46 - shortfall)` (dB of the viz kick band), `shortfall = max(0, -6 - sample_peak_dBFS)`. A quiet master is recognised from the overall level, never from the kick band (a kick-free song would otherwise lower the gate to its noise floor). Normal masters get the plan gate `max(-46, p95 - 30)`. A warning is written when a shortfall applies. (Revised after review R-DSP M1.) | interpretation |
| M1-S5 | **Hit events** = viz attack episodes that reach 0.3 (viz's one-hit-per-note re-arm ignored, so a strong kick 120 ms after a weak one is kept) or weaker attacks (≥ 0.3 × typical) with a ≥ 6 dB crisp onset; weak kick-band hits must also look like a drum kick (`kick_conf` ≥ 0.75). `t` = the largest step of a 400 Hz zero-phase dB envelope (low band ≤ 400 Hz plus 0.5 × the ≥ 1 kHz click band, 10/5 ms step contrast) in [peak − 75 ms, peak + 5 ms]; `t_peak` = the envelope maximum within 60 ms. | interpretation |
| M1-S6 | `hits.*` / `hits.*_raw` channels: the viz raw envelope is shifted earlier by the median detection lag (frames, recorded in provenance) and flashed at every event (contract flash rule) with the event's envelope value; the released curve is recomputed from it with the 0.18 s release. Values stay in 0..1 (viz clamps); the registry range 0..1.5 is unused headroom. | interpretation |
| M1-S7 | Event `strength` = attack / typical attack, capped at 2.0 (`1 ~ a typical hit`). | interpretation |
| M1-S8 | Additive optional fields on `hits.{kick,mid,high}` items: `env` (envelope value at the hit), `rise_db` (onset step, dB), `viz` (viz's own causal logic registers it: filter on it for viz-identical semantics); `hits.kick` also `kick_conf` (0..1: broadband click + decaying low band; sustained bass notes read low). | additive |
| M1-S9 | `onsets.*.strength` = the viz strength mapped to 0..1: `(viz − base) / (1 − base)` with base 0.55 (bass) / 0.6 (mid, high), so lib's vizState mapping (`0.55 + 0.45 s`, `0.6 + 0.4 s`) reproduces viz exactly. Onset `t` is refined on the same 400 Hz envelopes (search [flux peak − 100 ms, + 20 ms]). | interpretation |
| M1-S10 | `flux.*` = the viz flux (mean positive change of the normalised bands over the viz flux ranges 32-260 / 300-2200 / 2500-14000 Hz), p02-p99.5 per song. `timbre.slope_db_oct` = least squares of the per-bin power density (dB) of the 128 log bands vs log2 f over 100 Hz-10 kHz (white 0, pink −3 dB/oct). Centroid / roll-off / flatness over 20 Hz-20 kHz. Silent frames: timbre 0, width 0, balance 0, correlation 1. | interpretation |
| M1-S11 | Normalised twins (p02-p99.5) exclude frames at the dB floor (silence) when at least 1 % of the frames are above it. `norm.lo/hi` recorded per channel. | interpretation |

## loudness

| # | Decision | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| M1-L1 | `curve_offsets_s` = `{momentary: 0.2, short_term: 1.5}`: the curves are centred; a spec (trailing-window) meter shows `curve(t)` at `t + offset`. | interpretation of an optional field |
| M1-L2 | `true_peak_dbtp` = own 4× (2× at 96 kHz) polyphase oversampling (Kaiser β 10), max over channels, never below the sample peak; ffmpeg `ebur128=peak=true` is recorded in `ebur128.true_peak_dbtp` (0.1 dB resolution) with a warning beyond 0.5 dB. Integrated loudness: pyloudnorm (BS.1770-4); LRA: own EBU Tech 3342 implementation (P95 − P10 of 3 s blocks every 0.1 s, −70/−20 LU gates). | interpretation |
| M1-L3 | Mono is one channel (no dual-mono +3 dB); 5.1 drops the LFE and weights the surrounds 1.41 (BS.1770). | interpretation |
| M1-L4 | `clipped_samples` = samples (all channels) with \|x\| ≥ 1 − 2⁻¹⁵. | interpretation |
| M1-L5 | Additive optional keys in `meta/loudness.json`: `lra_threshold_lufs`, `short_term_percentiles {p10, p50, p90, p95}`, `loudest {t, short_term_lufs, rel_lu}`, `quietest {...}` (lowest short-term value whose whole 3 s window has sound), `momentary_max {t, lufs}`, `limited_master` (LRA < 7 LU and PLR < 9 dB), `qa[]` (`loudness:limited_master`, `loudness:clipping`, `loudness:ebur128_mismatch`, `loudness:silent`), `method {...}`. | additive |
| M1-L6 | `loud.rms_db` / `loud.peak_db` / `loud.crest_db` use the spectral analyser size (2048 samples at 44.1/48 kHz = 46 ms), centred; crest is 0 in silence. | interpretation |

## dynamics

| # | Decision | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| M1-D1 | `events/dynamics.json` parts cover the whole song contiguously. `level`: `silence` for detected silences, else by `rel_lu` (mean short-term LUFS − integrated) against thresholds −12 / −6 / −2 / +2 LU, **scaled** by `clip(range / 8 LU, 0.5, 1)` where range = P95 − P10 of the gated short-term curve (so a limited master still shows its own louder/quieter parts; the scale is in `meta/dynamics.json`). For a silence part `rel_lu`/`mean_lufs` use the momentary power mean. | interpretation |
| M1-D2 | Boundaries: Foote novelty (±4 s and ±8 s Gaussian checkerboards) on a 10 Hz robust-standardised feature matrix (short-term LU, bass and kick dB, kick and onset density, brightness, slope, width; silences held), peaks with prominence ≥ 0.35 × the median of the 8 strongest; parts > 30 s are split at their own strongest peak when the feature contrast is high enough; silences, stops, major impacts and long decay tails are fixed boundaries. Boundaries snap to downbeats (event edges only within 0.2 s). Short parts (< 2 bars, 3-6 s) and similar same-level neighbours are merged towards ~1 part per 20 s. Fallback when too few parts: allin1 sections (needs CR-M1-1) else a 4-bar grid scored by feature contrast (`fallback:dynamics:bar-grid`). | interpretation |
| M1-D3 | Additive optional fields on `dynamics` items: `energy` (mean `dyn.intensity`), `bass_db`, `kick_rate` (1/s), `onset_rate` (1/s), `brightness_hz`, `trend_lu_per_s`, `role_hint` (`drop` \| `build` \| `breakdown` \| null) and `role_score` — **evidence only**; EDM roles stay defined by structure (PLAN §5). | additive |
| M1-D4 | Impacts: score = max(ΔLU/4, ΔbassdB/6 (9 without a ≥ 1 LU rise), hit/6 LU) + brightness bonus; ≥ 1 with prominence 0.3, ≥ 1 s apart, not in the first 2 s of sound; `t` = the transient (kick / mid / high onset) with the largest 5 ms level jump around the peak. `strength = clip(0.15 + 0.3·score, 0, 1)`; `delta_lu` (momentary, 0.25-1.5 s after vs before), `delta_bass_db` (bass band, 0.1-1.5 s). `bar` = the bar containing t (null outside bars). Additive optional fields `after_silence`, `major` (ends a silence, or scores ≥ half the song's largest: never merged across). | interpretation + additive |
| M1-D5 | Silences: momentary below `max(min(I − 30, −45), −69.5)` LUFS for ≥ 0.5 s (lead-in/run-out ≥ 1 s), edges re-measured on a 5 ms RMS envelope of the mix (`ctx.audio('mix')`, D8). `depth_db` = integrated − median momentary inside. Additive `edge`, `digital`. | interpretation + additive |
| M1-D6 | Stops: a fall of ≥ 15 dB of the 5 ms level within 150 ms from a passage ≥ I − 12 LU into a gap below I − 20 LU lasting ≥ 0.25 s (every abrupt silence is a stop). `strength` = (level before − level in the gap) / 40 dB; `gap_s` = gap length. Additive `drop_db`, `to_silence`. | interpretation + additive |
| M1-D7 | Decay tails: after a hit, ≥ 1 s without a re-exciting attack while the 50 ms-smoothed level falls ≥ 10 dB at 3-80 dB/s (linear fit R² ≥ 0.5); ends at the next attack, 35 dB below the hit, or the song end. `strength` = hit level vs integrated. Additive `rate_db_s`. | interpretation + additive |
| M1-D8 | `dyn.intensity` = 0.45·clip((ST − I + 18)/22) + 0.2·bass + 0.2·(kick + onset density) + 0.15·brightness (the last three p02-p98 per song), 0.5 s zero-phase Hann, 0 in digital silence. `dyn.novelty` = the novelty above (10 Hz, interpolated to 100 Hz, max-normalised). | interpretation |
| M1-D9 | New optional file `meta/dynamics.json` (dynamics, schema none yet): thresholds and scale, method, `level_seconds`, `loudest_parts`, `quietest_parts`, counts, role hints — a readable answer to "where are the loud and quiet parts". | additive (new optional file) |

  is if other producers need headroom.

| M1-S6 | hits.kick events (review B1 / R2): the low (<= 400 Hz) band's own attack decides, not the >= 1 kHz click. Viz kick-band attack episodes are timed on the low band's step (the click step only breaks ties among steps >= half the largest, so a hat cannot take the timing); a hit within kick_refractory_s (0.2 s) of the previous event needs the own attack (step >= kick_own_step_db and excess >= kick_own_excess_db over the level 60..7 ms before); weak hits need a pitch drop (kick_glide >= kick_glide_min); low-band steps without a viz attack (fast double kicks) are kicks when the kick band is above its gate and they have a click >= kick_click_db plus a neighbour within kick_roll_s, or a click plus a strong pitch drop (kick_glide_hi). Params kick_click_w / kick_click_db(old meaning) removed. `viz` flags what viz's one-hit-per-note logic also registered; events viz registers on bass beating or hats without a low-band attack are no longer emitted, so the viz-flagged count is up to ~35 % below the viz reference on bass-heavy songs. | interpretation |
| M1-D3 | dynamics (review M2): a part's start boundary created by an impact keeps `t_event` (the unsnapped impact time); role_hints credits impacts within 0.6 s of the part start OR of t_event, so the drop hint survives the downbeat snap. | interpretation |
